# 🧋 Boba Bash — Discover Your Boba Personality

**Your vibe. Your flavor. Your story.**

Boba Bash is a fun, interactive personality quiz that matches your vibe with a boba tea personality! Answer playful questions, discover your boba alter ego, and explore the different personalities with your friends.


## 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5** — Website structure and content
- **CSS3** — Dark theme, layouts, responsive design, and animations
- **JavaScript** — Quiz questions, scoring, and result generation
